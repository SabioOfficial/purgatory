In the distant land, there was a place with wilted trees and unknown creatures. They roam freely across the **biome**, seeking **blood** and **flesh** from wanderers who unfortunately come across the land by **mere accident**.

<hr/>

This mod adds a new biome by the name of Purgatory that has dangerous entities, unique mechanics and custom potions.

**Recipe for Fearful Potion**
Awkward Potion + Sinful Dust

**Recipe for Potion of Sin**
Fearful Potion + Eye of Sin
